## Route

This zone has to Two Orientations Right Side Entrance and Right Side Entrance.
The zone has a simple explanation that works for 80% of the layouts and that is you go Left first and turn back on the ledge.
For a detailed read check individual layouts general rule works the same with some slight variations to bring it from 80% to 100% right
